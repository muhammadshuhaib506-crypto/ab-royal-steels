Our Process

Seven clear steps from your first enquiry to material delivered on site.

How we work

A supply workflow built for accuracy.

Each step has one job: make sure what arrives on site is exactly what the project needs.

  1. 01

    Understanding Customer Requirements

    We start by listening: the application, quantities, timelines, site conditions and any drawings or specifications you already have.

  2. 02

    Product Selection & Consultation

    We help you choose the right products, sizes and finishes for the job, and suggest alternatives where they make more sense.

  3. 03

    Material Specifications & Quotation

    You receive a clear quotation listing material, grade, dimensions, quantities and delivery terms, so there are no surprises.

  4. 04

    Order Confirmation

    Once you approve the quotation, we confirm the order in writing with agreed specifications, schedule and payment terms.

  5. 05

    Quality Inspection

    Material is checked against the order for specification, dimensions and visible surface condition before it is released.

  6. 06

    Packaging & Dispatch

    Material is bundled, strapped and labelled for safe handling, then loaded and dispatched on schedule.

  7. 07

    Transportation & Delivery

    We coordinate transport to your site and keep you informed until the material is delivered.

Start with step one.

Share your requirement and we will take it from there.
