Quality checkpoints

What we check on every order.

Review these checkpoints and edit them to match your inspection practice.

  1. Specification review

    Grade, size, thickness and finish confirmed with you before quotation.

  2. Visual inspection

    Surface condition checked for visible defects, rust and damage.

  3. Dimension check

    Key dimensions and thickness checked against the order.

  4. Quantity & identification

    Counts, weights and markings verified against the order.

  5. Packing check

    Bundles strapped and protected for safe handling in transit.

  6. Dispatch verification

    Load and documents checked before the vehicle leaves.
